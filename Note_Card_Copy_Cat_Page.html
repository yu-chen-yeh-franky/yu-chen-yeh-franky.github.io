Note Card Copy Cat

Reorderable note cards with one-tap copy, for iPhone, iPad & Android

App Store Download Google Play Download

Write ideas on cards, copy them anywhere, just like real life. Note Card Copy Cat is a free, offline note card app: each idea lives on its own card, you drag cards into the order you want, and one tap copies a card, or every card at once, to the clipboard. No account, no cloud, no ads.

How Note Card Copy Cat Works

  1. Add a card. Tap Add and type a phrase, a list item, an address, a code, anything you reuse.
  2. Arrange the stack. Drag cards up or down to put them in the order you need.
  3. Copy in one tap. Tap a card's copy button and paste it into any app. Use Copy All to grab the whole stack as one text block.
  4. Clean up. Swipe left on a card to delete it.

Why Choose Note Card Copy Cat

  • One idea per card: simpler than a long note, easier to rearrange than a list.
  • Copy, not retype: reusable templates, canned replies and codes are always one tap away.
  • Copy All: turn an ordered stack of cards into a message, list or document instantly.
  • 100% offline: everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud sync.
  • Free forever: no ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases.
  • Private: the developer collects no data.
  • Runs everywhere: iPhone, iPad, Apple silicon Mac, Apple Vision Pro and Android.

Who Is Note Card Copy Cat For?

  • People who paste the same text every day: support replies, email signatures, addresses, order numbers, promo codes.
  • List makers: shopping lists and to-do items you can reorder as priorities change.
  • Writers and brainstormers who think in fragments and shuffle them into shape.
  • Anyone who wants a notes app with no sign-up, no sync and nothing to configure.
