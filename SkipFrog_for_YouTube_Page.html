SkipFrog for YouTube

Skip & loop custom segments in YouTube videos. Free Chrome extension.

Chrome Web Store

SkipFrog lets you mark any part of a YouTube video as "skip" or "loop". Playback jumps past the parts you never want to hear again, such as a weak track in a two-hour DJ mix, and repeats the parts you love. Rules are saved per video, stored locally, and never get in the way when you seek manually.

How to Skip Part of a YouTube Video with SkipFrog

  1. Open a YouTube video in Chrome and click the SkipFrog frog icon in the toolbar.
  2. Set point A and point B. Type the times, or press A = current and B = current while the video plays to grab the exact spots.
  3. Click Add rule. Add as many rules as you like, and choose skip or loop for each.
  4. Sit back. When playback reaches A, the video jumps to B. Come back tomorrow and the rules are still there.

Why Use SkipFrog

  • Skip what you hate: remove filler tracks from DJ mixes, compilations and playlists-in-one-video.
  • Loop what you love: repeat a chorus, a guitar riff, a dance move or a tutorial step on demand.
  • Unlimited rules per video, mixing skip and loop behaviors.
  • Remembers every video: rules are stored per video ID and load automatically.
  • Never hijacks you: triggers only on natural playback. Manual seeking and paused videos are left alone.
  • Lightweight and private: runs only on youtube.com, stores everything locally, collects nothing.
  • Free, no ads, no account.

Who Is SkipFrog For?

  • Music fans who listen to long mixes and compilations but always skip the same few tracks.
  • Learners and musicians who need to replay a short passage of a lesson, speech or song.
  • Dancers and athletes drilling a specific move from a tutorial video.
  • Anyone rewatching long-form content who wants to trim intros, outros and sponsor segments.
