Silent haptic vibration timer for Apple Watch

App Store Download

VibeCraft Timer is a free, standalone Apple Watch timer that ends with a gentle haptic vibration instead of a loud alarm. Choose the intensity, preview it with a 3-second test, and let your wrist tell you when time is up. No iPhone needed, no data collected. Ideal for meditation, naps, focus sessions and quiet rooms.

How VibeCraft Timer Works

  1. Set the duration. Pick how long you want to meditate, nap or focus.
  2. Set the vibration. Tap Set Vibration and choose the intensity that feels right on your wrist.
  3. Test it. Run the 3-second test timer to preview the haptic before you commit.
  4. Start and relax. When time is up, your Apple Watch vibrates silently. No sound, no disturbing anyone.

Why Choose a Vibration Timer on Apple Watch

  • Silent by design: haptic feedback only, so the alarm never disturbs a quiet room, a sleeping partner or a meeting.
  • Adjustable intensity: from a barely-there tap to a firm buzz.
  • Test mode: preview the vibration in 3 seconds before starting a long session.
  • Adjust mid-session: change settings during an active countdown.
  • Standalone: runs entirely on the watch, no iPhone nearby required.
  • Smooth Mode (Premium): a one-time US$2.99 upgrade makes the vibration ramp up gradually, perfect for waking from a nap gently.
  • Private and free: no data collection, no ads, no subscription.

Who Is VibeCraft Timer For?

  • Meditators who want to be eased out of a session rather than jolted by a bell.
  • Power nappers who need a wake-up cue that will not wake anyone else.
  • Focus and Pomodoro workers in offices, libraries or shared spaces.
  • Anyone who prefers haptic cues over sound, including noisy environments where an alarm would go unheard.
