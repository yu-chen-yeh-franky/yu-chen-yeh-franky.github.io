How the Princess Card Trick Works

  1. Show six cards. Ask your friend to silently pick one and remember it. They never say it out loud.
  2. Flip and shuffle. The app turns the cards face-down and mixes them.
  3. They remove one card. Your friend takes away any single card.
  4. Reveal the rest. Show the remaining five cards one by one. The card they memorized has vanished.
